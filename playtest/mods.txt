Create: Golemancy 0.1.0-rc1 - playtest mod list
Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.266 (any 21.1.221 or newer works; Iron's Lib needs at least 21.1.221)

Put every jar below into the instance's mods folder. Versions are the ones Golemancy was built and
tested against (see docs/VERSIONS.md). "File" is the file name on the download page.

REQUIRED
--------
Create: Golemancy 0.1.0-rc1
    File:  golemancy-0.1.0-rc1.jar   (in this folder)

Create 6.0.10
    File:  create-1.21.1-6.0.10.jar
    Page:  https://modrinth.com/mod/create/versions?g=1.21.1&l=neoforge
           https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create
    Note:  Ponder, Flywheel and Registrate are bundled inside the Create jar.

Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 1.21.1-3.16.3
    File:  irons_spellbooks-1.21.1-3.16.3.jar
    Page:  https://modrinth.com/mod/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/versions?g=1.21.1&l=neoforge
           https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/irons-spells-n-spellbooks

Iron's Lib 1.21.1-2.3.0            (required by Iron's Spells)
    File:  irons_lib-1.21.1-2.3.0.jar
    Page:  https://modrinth.com/mod/irons-lib/versions?g=1.21.1&l=neoforge
